About the client

Leaksy is a content protection platform that scans millions of URLs across leak sites and fires DMCA takedowns automatically — 24/7, with no effort required from the creator. The product needed a site that matched its technical ambition.

What we built

A full marketing site and custom customer dashboard with login system — built entirely from scratch. The dashboard gives users real-time visibility into scans, leaks found, and takedown status. Dark, high-trust design language that speaks directly to the SaaS audience.

HTML · CSS · Vanilla JS · Custom auth & dashboard · Cloudflare Pages
