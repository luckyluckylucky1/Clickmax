What we do

Specialist care for active bodies.

Whether you're chasing a personal best, recovering from surgery, or just want to play with the kids without pain — we build a plan around your goals, not a template.

  • Sports injury rehab

    Hamstring strains, ACL recovery, ankle sprains, tennis elbow — assessed, taped, and rebuilt. Hands-on treatment paired with progressive loading to get you back stronger than before.

    Runners · footballers · weekend warriors

  • Strength & conditioning

    Force-plate testing, gym-based rehabilitation, and return-to-sport programs designed by physios who train alongside you. Bridge the gap between recovery and full performance.

    Post-injury athletes · gym-goers

  • Movement assessment

    A 60-minute biomechanical screen: gait analysis, joint range, control, and load tolerance. We find the cause, not just treat the pain — so it doesn't keep coming back.

    Nagging injuries · return-to-sport

  • Manual therapy

    Joint mobilisation, soft tissue release, dry needling, and kinesio taping. The hands-on side of treatment — used precisely, never as a long-term crutch.

    Acute pain · stiffness · recovery

  • Performance testing

    VALD ForceDecks, isometric strength benchmarks, asymmetry mapping. Quantify where you're at and track real progress — guesswork has no place in your rehab.

    Elite athletes · ACL return-to-play

  • General physiotherapy

    Back pain, neck pain, headaches, post-surgical rehab, work injuries. Sports is our specialty, but we treat anyone who walks, lifts, or sits at a desk — basically, everyone.

    Everyday aches · chronic pain · WorkCover

Meet the owner

Marcus Hale

Owner and principal physiotherapist at Tallowood, Marcus has spent over a decade working with elite athletes — including AFL clubs, Athletics Australia, and local Mulgrave footy and netball teams. He started Tallowood in 2021 to bring that same level of care to the community he grew up in.

"Sports physio isn't just about getting you out of pain. It's about getting you back to the thing you love, and making sure you stay there."
Qualifications Masters of Sports Physio, La Trobe
