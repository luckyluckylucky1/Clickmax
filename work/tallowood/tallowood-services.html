What we treat

Sports physiotherapy,
and everything around it.

Sports is our specialty — but a body is a body, and we treat the whole person. Below is a full list of what we offer in clinic. If you can't see what you need, just ask.

Our approach

Built around sport. Open to everyone.

Every Tallowood physio holds postgraduate sports qualifications. That depth shapes how we treat all our patients — whether you're an elite footballer or a desk worker with a sore shoulder.

Sessions are 30 minutes for follow-ups, 60 minutes for initial consults. Plenty of time to assess properly, treat hands-on, and leave you with a plan.

ACL & knee reconstruction rehab

Full pre-op and post-op pathway, from week-one swelling control through to return-to-sport testing. We work to RTS criteria, not just timelines.

Footy · netball · soccer

Hamstring & calf injuries

Acute strain assessment, graded loading programs, and the conditioning work that actually prevents the next one. The most common injury we see — and one we know cold.

Sprinting · running · AFL

Ankle sprains & instability

From rolled ankles on the basketball court to chronic instability. Bracing, balance retraining, and strength work to stop the cycle of re-injury.

Basketball · netball · trail running

Shoulder injuries (overhead athletes)

Rotator cuff tears, labral injuries, dislocations, throwing shoulder. Specialist assessment for swimmers, cricketers, tennis players, and weightlifters.

Cricket · swimming · tennis

Tendinopathies (Achilles, patellar, glute med)

Stubborn, slow-burn injuries that need a structured loading program — not rest. We build the plan, then progressively load it through to performance.

Running · jumping sports

Concussion management

Sport-related concussion assessment and graduated return-to-play. Working alongside GPs and sports physicians where needed.

Contact sports · adolescent athletes

Pre-season screening & injury prevention

Movement screen, strength asymmetry mapping, and a flagged risk profile before your season starts. We work with several local clubs at squad level.

Clubs · individuals · all ages

Performance & testing

Click to read more

VALD ForceDecks strength testing

Force-plate isometric and jump testing. We measure left-right asymmetry, peak force, rate of force development, and reactive strength — and use the data to drive your program.

RTS · benchmarking

Running biomechanics analysis

Video gait assessment with slow-motion playback. Identify cadence, contact time, and movement faults that contribute to recurring lower-limb pain.

Runners · marathoners · triathletes

Return-to-sport testing

The objective gate before you go back to competition. Hop tests, strength benchmarks, and sport-specific movement criteria — pass them, then play.

Post-ACL · post-surgery

Strength & conditioning programming

Programs written by physios, supervised in-clinic or delivered for your gym. Built specifically around your injury history and your sport.

Individuals · post-rehab

Manual therapy

Joint mobilisation & manipulation

Targeted hands-on techniques to restore movement at stiff joints. Used as part of treatment, never as a standalone fix.

Spine · shoulder · hip

Soft tissue release & deep tissue work

Trigger point release, fascial work, and post-game recovery massage. Useful for acute tightness and complementing your training load.

Recovery · pre-comp

Dry needling

Western-style needling for muscular pain and trigger points. Performed by all our physios with postgraduate certification in dry needling.

Neck · back · calf

Kinesio taping & rigid taping

Targeted taping to support injured structures during training and competition. We'll teach you how to do it yourself for the away games.

Match-day · in-season
