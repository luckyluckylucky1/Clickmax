Angel Face Aesthetics

About the client

Angel Face Aesthetics is a boutique lash, brow and beauty studio. The brief was simple: build something that matched the elegance of Jade's work — refined, feminine, and effortless to book through.

What we built

A fully custom site centred around the studio's identity — editorial serif typography, a soft cream palette, and a hand-drawn lash illustration woven through the hero. The existing Acuity booking system was integrated directly so clients can book without ever leaving the site.

HTML · CSS · Vanilla JS · Acuity Scheduling · Cloudflare Pages
