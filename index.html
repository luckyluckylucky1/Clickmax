What makes a great business website

We build sites that actually work for your business.

A site isn't just something that exists — it's a tool that should bring people in, answer their questions, and make them want to get in touch. Here's what that looks like in practice:

01

Copy that converts

Every page is written to speak to the people who actually land on it — not stuffed with keywords or recycled from a competitor's site. Clear, direct, and shaped around what your customers need to know before they pick up the phone.

02

Integrations that fit your workflow

Booking systems, contact forms, maps, live chat, payment links — wired directly into the site so it works with your existing tools. We never tell you to switch platforms; we build around what you already use.

03

Built to update easily

Prices change. Hours change. New products launch. Sites we build are structured so the things that change most often are the quickest to update — one message and it's done, no CMS gymnastics required.

04

Speed that actually matters

Over half your visitors will leave if your site takes more than three seconds to load. Every Clickmax build loads in under one — on mobile, on slow connections, from the other side of the country. No plugins, no bloat, just clean code.
